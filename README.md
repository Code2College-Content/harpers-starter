# Harper's Home Care: family updates (practice project)

Client: Janelle Harper, owner and care director (fictional).

## The slice for Sessions 9 to 12

1. **Comparison** (comparison.md): the three options on data/options_sheet.csv, compared on Janelle's questions. Every box says where it came from: the options sheet or an interview card.
2. **Recommendation** (recommendation.md): one option, a few sentences, citing the sheet and the interviews, with its weak spot named.
3. **One screen clickable mockup** (mockup.html) of the recommended option: what a family member sees after a visit. Made up client, no real login.

Solo version: the comparison and the recommendation (no mockup).

## What is here

* data/options_sheet.csv: the three options (the costs are made up for this project)
* interviews/: 4 caregiver cards and 2 family cards
* comparison.md, recommendation.md, mockup.html: stubs
* index.html: the team home page your link opens

## Rule for this project
Use only the options sheet and the interview cards. No outside products, prices or websites. If a number is not on the sheet, write "not known yet".

## Before you start

* This repo is **public**. Everything in it is about a fictional business made up for practice.
* Never put personal information here: no real names, phone numbers, addresses or passwords.
* Never paste anything private into Copilot. Describe the columns instead of pasting rows.
* When you leave, save, commit and sync. In a Codespace, also stop it (Codespaces menu, bottom left, Stop Current Codespace). On your own laptop there is nothing to stop.

## Team rules

1. One person per file. Shared files (index.html, site.css, .github/copilot-instructions.md) change only when the team agrees.
2. Sync before you start. Commit and sync when you stop.
